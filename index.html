The crane, whale, butterfly, and frog are traditional origami designs, passed down through generations of folders around the world.

The 3D step-by-step lessons in this app were built from scratch to teach these classic models — every fold was placed by hand in the lesson data.

Thank you

Thank you for folding with me.

Origami has been one of my greatest teachers. It asks for patience, precision, and presence — the same qualities I try to bring to movement practice.

Every crease is a small conversation between your hands and the paper. I built this app to share that conversation with you.

Keep folding, keep breathing, keep playing.
