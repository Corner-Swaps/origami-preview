Origami tutorials adapted from Origami Odyssey by Robb Doering.
https://github.com/robbwdoering/origamiodyssey

The source crease geometry and folding instructions are adapted into our own
connected-panel animation engine. Numerical corrections and intermediate
motions are adaptations, not an endorsement by the original author.
These are authored tutorials, not a trained machine-learning model.

FOLD format: Erik Demaine, Jason Ku and Robert Lang.
https://github.com/edemaine/fold

Manta Ray is a separately authored native animation adaptation.
Reference design: Nick Robinson, https://origami.me/manta-ray/
Its final shaping differs from the reference. No reference images or videos
are bundled. The Origami Odyssey MIT notice above applies to that source,
not to third-party reference materials.

Dragonfly is a locally authored bird-base cut-and-fold variation. Its first
24 preparation actions use the MIT-licensed Origami Odyssey source above.
Its finishing differs from the traditional reference. It has two late wing
slits and removes no paper outline.

The previous six-cut Scorpion was incorrect and has been withdrawn. The
replacement must use no cuts and is not currently available. Marc Vigo's
reference diagrams and author-specific finishing are not bundled.

Whale is an independently authored 42-action, uncut bird-base adaptation.
Design reference: Joost Langeveld, https://joostlangeveldorigami.nl/diagramswhale.html
The 24-action foundation uses Origami Odyssey (Robb Doering, MIT).
Fin-tip tucks and individually raised tail layers differ from the reference's
inside reverse folds. The final two body-opening folds are original shaping,
producing separated sides and an open underside. This is an adaptation,
not an exact reconstruction.
No reference images, diagrams, videos or tutorial text are distributed.
